Why Use This Online Word Counter?

Whether you are a writer, student, or social media manager, tracking word count is essential for meeting content guidelines. This tool offers real-time calculations for word counts, total character limits, and estimated reading speeds.

How Reading Time is Calculated

The average reading speed for an adult is roughly 200 words per minute. Our algorithm estimates reading time by processing your word total against standard reading benchmarks.
